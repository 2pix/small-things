▲ Lies - the pieces that jump the reader (snakes and ladders) dashed - to write, architecture exists dotted - outline only coloured dot - the year in the story's time

The board is read like a board game: left to right along the bottom row, then back along the next, climbing. The year dots show the story's own time - the twelve year jump between 2014 and 2024 is real, and the reader feels it as the board thins. The Lies are the ladders: each one jumps the reader somewhere the scenes alone would not reach.
