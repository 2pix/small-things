---
title: Create from Nothing
subtitle: "1: On attention, exile, and returning to what is already yours"
category: black-cat
date: 2026-02-05
---

Three decades ago I stood at an intersection where petrochemical industry met residential, flanked by the Yarra River. A strip of grass between dusty road and water. Thirty meters long. Six meters wide.

I chose it because my body knew it was mine.

I gathered what I loved.

A fountain pen. Turquoise ink—the only pot I owned because that color made me alive. A worn brush. Water in a plastic bottle. An old sketchbook.

Not tools I was told to use.

Tools I already desired.

For four days I drew what was there.

The West Gate Bridge overhead, its traffic leaving gray mist on everything below. Weatherboard houses with brown gardens—neat but struggling, the kind that would need devotion to turn green.

Cold air. Autumn light, thin and unforgiving.

I made marks the ink wouldn't let me take back. Committed before I was certain. Watered down what went too far. Added hard lines where mistakes showed.

Turquoise holding the chill, the grim reality, the particular quality of light that only exists when something is ending and hasn't yet turned.

I was not performing.

I was attending.

I was sensing the in-between.

The body does not preserve what isn't real.

I remember it still. Vividly. The sheer of the cold air. The smell of industry meeting river. The exact angle of light on water.

Because I was present.

After that, I spent years away from this state.

Wearing borrowed frameworks. Living fractions of other people's lives. Performing competence in languages that weren't mine.

Borrowed lives began to calcify. The hollowing was the worst nightmare—devastation that turns brittle, like a writer gripping the page until it refuses to answer. Fluency hardened into mimicry. Motion continued, contact did not.

The disillusionment grew until it was louder than the performance.

Then I listened.

Exile is also fertile ground.

Information accumulates. Experience layers and thickens. Eventually the density becomes its own authority.

You don't waste suffering if you let it teach you where you abandoned yourself.

Creativity is not effort.

It is attention materialized.

It withdraws when I stop listening. It fades the moment curiosity tightens—when attention shifts from what is alive to what I think should appear. When I start watching myself instead of the work. When I try to recreate a moment instead of meeting the one that's here.

Curiosity is not effort. It is a posture. A willingness to stay with what I don't yet understand without rushing it into form. The body knows how to do this. It leans forward. It listens. It follows what pulls, not what performs.

She returns when I let longing take shape instead of managing it. When I stop waiting for the arrival of something and recognize that I am already inside the encounter. She feeds what is present, not what was successful. She doesn't repeat outcomes. She repeats contact.

There is no discipline here.

Only the refusal to abandon the conversation.

I don't need to return to Melbourne.

I am returning to the state where I pay attention without asking first. Where I trust what I'm drawn to without needing validation to justify the choice. Where I use what I already love because devotion is the only authority required.

Living itself becomes the art when you stop performing and start materializing.

Spirit insisting on flesh.

Attention becoming form.

This is how you create from nothing:

You don't.

You create from everything you already are when you finally stop borrowing another person's yes.
