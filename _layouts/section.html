// BLACK CAT

Updated: 17 Oct 2026 - A friend said these pieces read more like scenes in a play. The lies can be read like a lone voice in the dark. The scenes form whole in my mind's eye, complete with sound, smell and taste. The lies are an inversion. Something I have believed for so long that became the floor. Something I didn't question. Until of course, it falls apart.

The politics in these pieces live in bodies, not in arguments. What a body keeps doing. Over time. What it holds for other people, what it holds for the characters themselves, without them knowing. And what it refuses. Until it's revealed.

// THE FLOOR

The floor used to be a plane where what I believed in was held securely. It looked and felt like a floor. What I am discovering is that there are levels below that. Sometimes, I stumble on a hidden door leading below. Sometimes, the ground cracks and I fall onto the cold ground below.

I've realised the place I used to know as the floor runs on a transaction: belong by producing, by climbing, by being useful, by paying.

The floor I am beginning to understand is where we can lie down and notice where my back is not meeting the ground. Where that particular kink in the muscle is. What we are holding and have not let go of. A place where belonging stops being conditional.

Some of what I found is here.

// ART OF SMALL THINGS

What is the smallest thing you can do today just for yourself? And it doesn't have to be a fancy paid thing. It can be small like clearing your drawer (smaller than the whole cupboard) or a walk. Or a meal. Or a meal with a friend. If being with family is too close for comfort. On the same day each month. A group chat where members actually respond when something is hard.

I think this is what held communities together before villages became cities and there were programs for it. Before organising. Just following an impulse to connect, with ourselves and an other. To make right (not perfect). To be heard and held. And even if it is to "fight" (I am not condoning violence, or use of force. What gets suppressed over time does explode.)
