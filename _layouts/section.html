I worked at a caregiving agency.

Took the calls with employers. Sat with the caregivers. Asked the questions. I ask a lot of questions.

After a while, I started noticing what everyone was carrying.

So I ran the numbers.

Some of what I found is here.

{% when "the-art-of-small-things" %}

The smallest unit is a small group.

Two people. Three. A meal on the same day each month. A walk. A message thread that actually responds when something is hard.

That's what held communities together before there were programs for it.

Art of Small Things is about rebuilding that — not at scale, not with a curriculum. In your own life, with people you already half-know. For caregiving. For whatever the people in the group need.

The groups that form here make their own rules. Their own rhythm. Their own vibe.

This is where you learn how to start one.
