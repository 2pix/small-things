Updated: 27 Sept 2026 - this body of work came in pieces. I started on a writing workshop and where prompts were given daily. I didn't always follow the prompts but it helped me to get started. The scenes form whole in my mind's eye, complete with sound, smell and taste.

A friend said these pieces read more like scenes in a play. The lies can be read like a lone voice in the dark.

The politics in these pieces live in bodies, not in arguments. What a body holds for other people, what it refuses, what it knows before anyone has a chance to think it out.

{% include line-map.html %} {% when "the-floor" %}

The floor is the place where belonging stops being conditional.

Everything above it runs on a transaction: belong by producing, by climbing, by being useful, by paying.

The floor is what we lie on to notice where our back has a kink and doesn't settle. What we are holding and have not let go of.

Some of what I found is here.

{% when "the-art-of-small-things" %}

The smallest unit is a small group.

Two people. Three. A meal on the same day each month. A walk. A message thread that actually responds when something is hard.

That's what held communities together before there were programs for it.

Art of Small Things is about rebuilding that — not at scale, not with a curriculum. In your own life, with people you already half-know. For caregiving. For whatever the people in the group need.

The groups that form here make their own rules. Their own rhythm. Their own vibe.

This is where you learn how to start one.
